Hook ideas by product, in the creator's brief

PROPOSED

The brief is the current creator mobile flow from Figma. One section is new: it sits between Campaign Brief and Brand Guidelines.

Swipe style

  • One product per tile, and the whole tile swipes. No card inside a card. Swipe or drag it sideways.
  • Side by side: the next product's tile peeks in at the edge of the screen.
  • Stacked deck: the other products sit behind the tile with their top edges showing. Pull left for the next product, right for the one before.
  • The next product's tile shows at the edge of the screen.
  • Three lines per list, then Show more. The link opens both lists on that tile, and the tile closes again when you leave it. Add ?lines=2 to the link to try another number.
  • Dots under the tile show where you are and jump when tapped. Arrow keys work too.
  • Creators see every product's hook ideas and instructions, not only their own.

Changed from Figma: its two products are both named Big Dulce. Here they carry the names on the packs, so the pages can be told apart.

Product names and the lines on the tiles are sample content.
